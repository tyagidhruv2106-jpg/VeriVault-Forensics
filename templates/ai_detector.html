Forensic Neural Architecture & Training Specifications

Model Backbone EfficientNet-B0 (ImageNet-1k Pretrained Transfer Learning)
Input Modality Dual-Stream 6-Channel Input Tensor (3-channel RGB + 3-channel ELA Compression Residuals)
Custom Classification Head AdaptiveAvgPool2d → Linear(1280, 256) → BatchNorm1d → SiLU → Dropout(p=0.4) → Linear(256, 2)
Explainability Method Grad-CAM (Gradient-Weighted Class Activation Mapping) on features[-1] conv layer
Augmentation & Regularization Random Horizontal Flip, Affine Rotation, JPEG Compression Simulation (Q=65–95), Label Smoothing
Optimizer & Scheduler AdamW (lr=3e-4, weight_decay=1e-4) with ReduceLROnPlateau & EarlyStopping
Target Forensics Domains Tiny-GenImage (Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, GANs) & CASIA 2.0 / CocoGlide (splicing & inpainting)
